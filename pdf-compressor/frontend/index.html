Understanding PDF Optimization

A Portable Document Format (PDF) file combines vector shapes, scalable typography, structural dictionaries, and bitmap imagery. Often, documents created by scanners or word processors include uncompressed photographs, duplicate font libraries, and redundant object streams that dramatically inflate file size.

PDFCompress Pro solves this by applying targeted optimizations:

🔤

Font Subsetting

Removes unused font characters so only the exact glyphs visible on your pages are stored in the file.

📐

Image DPI Resampling

Downsamples 300+ DPI images to screen-friendly resolutions (150 DPI or 110 DPI) using high-grade bicubic filtering.

🗜️

Flate & CCITT Stream Encoding

Compacts document content streams and applies specialized lossless compression for black-and-white scanned text.

Which Compression Level Is Right for You?

  • 🟢 Low (200+ DPI): Minimal compression. Best for official legal documents, graphic design proofs, and photo portfolios.
  • 🟡 Medium (150 DPI — Recommended): The ideal balance of size and visual fidelity. Perfect for resumes, business reports, and everyday sharing.
  • 🔴 High (110 DPI): Strong compression. Ideal for staying below strict email attachment limits (e.g., 10MB or 20MB).
  • ⚫ Extreme (75 DPI): Maximum size reduction for strict university or government application portals requiring files under 1MB.

Frequently Asked Questions

✅

Which compression level should I choose?

For everyday sharing and resumes, we recommend Medium (150 DPI) for a great reduction with no visible quality loss. Use High or Extreme when meeting strict portal upload limits.

📊

Why do scanned PDFs compress more?

Scanned PDFs contain uncompressed, high-DPI image bitmaps. Our engine resamples these images and applies specialized CCITT Fax and DCT compression, often shrinking files by 70% to 80%.

🔍

Will compression make my text blurry?

No. Our engine preserves native vector text and font streams as crisp vectors. Text is never converted into blurry pixel images.

🔒

Is my document secure and private?

Yes. All uploads are processed over encrypted SSL connections in isolated temporary memory and deleted immediately after processing. We never store or inspect your documents.

📱

Can I compress PDFs on my smartphone?

Yes! PDFCompress Pro works seamlessly on Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet) and iOS (Safari). Generated files open directly in native mobile viewers.

Yes. All features, compression modes, and downloads are 100% free with no account creation, watermarks, or hidden limits.
