Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At PDFCompress Pro, accessible from our website, the privacy and security of our visitors and their documents is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by PDFCompress Pro and how we use it.

1. Document Privacy & Ephemeral Processing (Zero File Storage)

PDFCompress Pro is an ephemeral utility. We believe that your documents belong strictly to you.

  • No Permanent Storage: Uploaded PDF files are held only temporarily in isolated system memory or temporary disk cache solely to execute the requested compression algorithm.
  • Immediate Automated Deletion: All temporary files are permanently destroyed and erased immediately after processing. We do not keep copies, backups, or archives of your uploaded documents.
  • No Content Inspection or AI Training: We never open, read, index, analyze, sell, or use your documents or text for machine learning or artificial intelligence model training.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All file transfers between your browser and our processing nodes are secured with Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit or use our services, we may collect minimal non-personally identifiable technical information:

  • Standard Web Server Logs: Like most standard websites, we automatically log basic access information, including your internet protocol (IP) address, browser user-agent, operating system, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps. This data is used solely to administer the site, prevent abuse, and optimize performance.
  • Aggregated Usage Analytics: We collect aggregate statistics such as total compressions processed, file size reduced, and error rates to monitor platform health and reliability.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

PDFCompress Pro uses standard cookies to ensure website functionality. In addition, third-party partners and advertising vendors may place cookies on your device:

  • Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our primary advertising partner is Google AdSense. Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on PDFCompress Pro, which are sent directly to users' browsers.

Note that PDFCompress Pro has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers. Please consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

For users situated in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights:

  • The right to access information we hold about you (if any).
  • The right to rectification of any inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure of personal data. (Because our document processing is completely ephemeral and does not retain documents, your files are erased immediately by design).
  • The right to restrict or object to data processing.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Disclosures

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose what personal information it collects, request the deletion of personal information, and request that a business not sell the consumer's personal data. PDFCompress Pro does NOT sell, rent, or trade user personal data or uploaded document contents.

7. Children's Privacy

PDFCompress Pro does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We encourage visitors to frequently check this page for any changes. Your continued use of this site after any change in this Privacy Policy will constitute your acceptance of such change.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our security practices, please reach out through our Contact Page.
